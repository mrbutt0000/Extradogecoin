Version 1.0 | October 2026

1. Executive Summary

Extra Doge Coin ($EDOGE) is a community-driven meme token. It is planned to launch on the Base network. It aims to blend internet meme culture with AI and gaming utilities, built on transparent tokenomics and a zero-tax design.

2. Tokenomics

Planned total supply: 510,000,000 $EDOGE (fixed).

  • 50% Liquidity Pool: 255,000,000 $EDOGE added to liquidity at launch, with LP lock proof to be published.
  • 10% Community Airdrop: 51,000,000 $EDOGE for early supporters and active participants.
  • 20% AI & Gaming Utility: 102,000,000 $EDOGE for ecosystem and utility development.
  • 20% Team, Marketing & Operations: 102,000,000 $EDOGE. Vesting / lock details will be published before launch.

3. Security & Transparency

$EDOGE is planned with a 0% buy and 0% sell tax. At launch, the supply is planned to be fixed with no mint function and ownership renounced, and proof (transaction links) will be published. Liquidity lock details and wallet addresses will be shared publicly after launch. No technology can remove all risk, so please do your own research.

4. Utility Vision

The team plans to develop AI-powered gaming utility for holders. These are goals, not guarantees, and development timelines may change.

5. Growth Approach

We focus on organic community growth, transparent communication and consistent updates rather than expensive paid promotion. Future exchange or listing plans depend on market conditions and platform requirements, and no price or return is promised.
